# CONTEXT — GBFR Sharecard

Ubiquitous language for the Granblue Fantasy: Relink build-sharecard domain.
Each entry says what the term means and what the Build stores; the mechanics
behind it live in [docs/](docs/).

## Glossary

- **Build** — one character's complete shareable loadout: weapon, skills, sigils, wrightstone, summons, over-masteries, master traits. One Build per Character (v1).
- **Character** — a GBFR character, identified by a character id. The roster names all 23; only those with a Character Catalog are playable, and the rest show as unselectable.
- **Character Catalog** — the per-character definition a Character needs to be playable: its skill list, style titles and master-trait cells. A Character without one is on the roster but cannot be built.
- **Trait** — a named effect (e.g. _Damage Cap_, _Stun Power_) with a game-defined max level. Traits are the currency of the whole model: sigils, wrightstones, weapons and the totals all speak in traits.
- **Sigil Slot** — one of the Build's 12 sigil positions: `{ primaryTrait, secondaryTrait?, level }`. Trait-based - the sigil _item_ is never named. A slot may be empty. The single `level` applies to both traits. See [docs/sigils.md](docs/sigils.md).
- **Primary Trait / Secondary Trait** — the two traits a sigil slot can carry; a secondary trait exists only on "+"-style sigils.
- **Masteries** — the per-character progression screen, bought with **MSP**. Four sections: **Offense**, **Defense**, **Collection** (one per weapon) and **Over Mastery**. Do not call it a "board", and do not confuse it with **Master Traits** or **Master Level**, separate systems that merely share the prefix. See [docs/masteries.md](docs/masteries.md).
- **MSP** — Mastery Points, the currency that buys Masteries, Master Levels and meditations alike. See [docs/masteries.md](docs/masteries.md).
- **Master Level** — the character's rank, capped at 55. Bought with MSP, not earned alongside Masteries; it grants flat stats plus the points that fund Master Traits. See [docs/master-traits.md](docs/master-traits.md).
- **Over Mastery** — the Masteries section holding exactly 4 fully-random bonus lines on a character: `{ bonusType, value }` each. Any Bonus Type can appear on any line; no line is fixed. A line may be empty (unrolled). See [docs/overmasteries.md](docs/overmasteries.md).
- **Bonus Type** — one of the 11 canonical over-mastery bonus kinds (Attack Power Up, Health Up, Critical Hit Rate Up, Stun Power Up, and the seven damage/cap lines). Summon equip bonuses draw on the same 11. See [docs/overmasteries.md](docs/overmasteries.md).
- **Wrightstone** — one optional equip per Build: a **main** slot and two **sub** slots, each `{ trait, level }`, traits drawn from the wrightstone trait pool. The wrightstone's display name is _derived_: its prefix follows the main trait (e.g. "Dread…" ⇔ Stun Power). See [docs/wrightstones.md](docs/wrightstones.md).
- **Summon Slot** — one of the Build's 4 summon positions: `{ summonId, trait, traitLevel, equipBonus }`. The rolled trait and rolled equip bonus are player-entered, since the rolls are random in-game. See [docs/summons.md](docs/summons.md).
- **Status** — the character's four headline stat values: HP, ATK, Crit. Hit Rate (%) and Stun Power. **Derived, never stored**: `deriveStatus` computes all four from the Build, assuming it is at cap throughout. See [docs/stats.md](docs/stats.md).
- **PWR** — the single power number the game prints beside Status. Not part of Status and **player-entered**: it is paid for by what a Build invests rather than by what it displays. See [docs/stats.md](docs/stats.md#pwr).
- **Weapon** — the Build's one equipped weapon: a name, a series, base HP and ATK, and five **weapon-trait slots**. Slots 1, 3 and 5 are fixed; slots 2 and 4 are **rollable** from a per-series pool the player chooses within. Everything but those pool choices is derived from the (character × series) catalog entry, since v1 shows only the maxed weapon. The Wrightstone renders as the weapon's "Imbued Traits" (game presentation) but stays its own Build field. See [docs/weapons.md](docs/weapons.md).
- **Weapon Series** — one of six trait-slot templates a weapon belongs to, named after its slot-1 trait: **Defender** (HP), **Stunner** (Stun Power), **Stinger** (Crit Rate), **Executioner** (Weak Point DMG), **Ascension** (ATK) and **Terminus** (Catastrophe). See [docs/weapons.md](docs/weapons.md).
- **Skill Slot** — one of the Build's 4 equipped skills, chosen from the character's skill catalog.
- **Trait Level Totals** — the per-trait _effective_ level, which is what the game counts and what Status reads. Levels from every source add, and the sum is left uncapped, so a total past the trait's max reads as an overcap; the trait's value ladder is then read once at that total. Shown in the editor's Trait Checklist, never on the card. See [docs/stats.md](docs/stats.md#trait-levels-pool-and-the-pooled-level-indexes-the-ladder-once).
- **Master Traits** — a character's three **Styles**, each progressing through four **Style Rank** sections (1 / 2 / 3 / EX). A separate system from **Masteries**; the archive calls it the `skillboard`. All selected traits across all three styles are active simultaneously. The Build stores, per style × rank, the _set of selected cell ids_; everything else is derived. See [docs/master-traits.md](docs/master-traits.md).
- **Style** — one of the three universal master-trait tracks: **Insight / Essence / Crux**. The words are the same for every character; each character gives its own **title** to each style, and the skill-related cells inside differ. ("Style" is the working term - the user reads it more as a "direction"; rename if a better word surfaces.)
- **Master-trait option** — one selectable _cell_ in a style×rank section. Its **OptionId names the cell, not the trait**: each cell either references a **generic** trait definition from a pool shared by every character (which may legitimately occupy two cells of the same section) or carries a **character-specific** skill-related definition inline. Cell order in the character's catalog _is_ the table layout.
- **Style Rank budget** — each rank section has a points pool shared across all three styles. Every selection costs exactly 1 point, so the budget is simply the max number of selections in that rank section. See [docs/master-traits.md](docs/master-traits.md#ranks-carry-the-budget).
- **Style Rank Perk** — the bonus a style grants at a Style Rank, activated by selecting enough of that rank's cells. Ranks 1, 2 and 3 each have one; EX has none. Perk active-state is **derived from selections, never stored**. For the thresholds and the rule, see [docs/master-traits.md](docs/master-traits.md#the-three-perks-are-the-styles-mechanic).
- **Card** — the read-only 1920×1080 render of a Build, exported as PNG. Shows the master-trait tables in their full 3-styles × 4-ranks selection structure rather than a flat list of selected traits.
- **Editor** — the interactive UI that mutates a Build; separate from the Card.
